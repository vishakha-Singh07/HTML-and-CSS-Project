A. P. J. ABDUL KALAM

1931 - 2015

ABOUT A. P. J. ABDUL KALAM

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam was one of the most respected scientists and leaders of India. He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He played an important role in India's space and missile development programs.

He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. Because of his great contribution to India's missile and space programs, he was popularly known as the "Missile Man of India".

Dr. Kalam was also a great teacher, writer and inspirational person. He always encouraged young people to dream big, work hard and contribute to the development of the nation.
